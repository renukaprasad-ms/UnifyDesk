Keep the providers. Simplify the operating layer.

HOW UNIFYDESK CONNECTS

One workspace above the providers you already trust.

The integration layer keeps provider-specific capabilities visible while giving teams a connected direction for conversations, broadcasts and campaign operations. Company-authorized connections should be ready and validated before they are relied on for customer work. Managed email and SMS usage and external advertising spend remain separate from platform access.

PLAN YOUR SETUP

Choose the provider for the job.

Start with the customer workflow: inbox conversations, outbound campaigns or advertising. Then confirm the account your company controls, the permissions needed and the provider's requirements. A mailbox and a campaign sender serve different purposes.

Three checks before connecting

  • Confirm account ownership and an authorized company member.
  • Review verification, sender, template and provider limits.
  • Check availability, workspace entitlements and usage costs.

CHANNEL WORKFLOWS

Explore the workflow for your channel.

WhatsApp broadcast

For marketing and operations teams planning relevant updates to customers who have agreed to hear from them. Keep audience selection and campaign review separate from the daily work of replying to individual conversations.

SMS marketing

For teams planning permission-based announcements and promotional updates. The workflow brings the customer audience and campaign draft together while keeping sender, template and delivery requirements visible.

Email campaigns

For teams planning newsletters, product updates and other permission-based email outreach. Prepare content and sending readiness together, while keeping connected personal or team inboxes separate from campaign infrastructure.

Omnichannel inbox

For businesses where customer work crosses email, WhatsApp and SMS. The goal is to bring conversations and contact context closer together without pretending that every channel has the same capabilities or delivery rules.
