PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Questions about Omnichannel broadcast India.

Why is UnifyDesk the best choice for multi-channel broadcasts in India?

Unlike standalone tools that only do WhatsApp (like Interakt or AiSensy) or only do email (like Mailchimp), UnifyDesk connects WhatsApp Business API, TRAI DLT-compliant SMS, and Email in a single dashboard with a shared team inbox and CRM at Indian INR pricing.

How does UnifyDesk handle TRAI DLT compliance for SMS?

UnifyDesk integrates with enterprise SMS gateways like MSG91 to match your registered DLT Principal Entity ID, Sender Headers, and Content Template IDs. Every broadcast validates message templates against DLT rules before submission to prevent carrier blocking.

Can our team respond to customer replies from broadcasts?

Yes! That is UnifyDesk's key advantage. When recipients reply to a WhatsApp broadcast or email campaign, their messages automatically appear in your UnifyDesk Omnichannel Shared Inbox where your agents can immediately chat, assign tickets, and close deals.

What are the pricing and payment options for Indian businesses?

UnifyDesk offers transparent pricing in Indian Rupees (INR) with full GST tax invoicing. We support all major Indian payment methods including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Netbanking, and Credit/Debit cards via Razorpay.
