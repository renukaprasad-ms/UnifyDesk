ABOUT UNIFYDESK

Customer work should feel like one system.

UnifyDesk is being built around a simple idea: communication, outreach and advertising should not force a growing business to operate from a collection of disconnected dashboards.

Why we're building it

Businesses add inboxes, delivery providers, campaign tools and advertising platforms as they grow. Each solves a problem, but the customer context gets fragmented between them.

What we're changing

UnifyDesk creates a common operating layer across those workflows while allowing the underlying providers to keep doing the jobs they are good at.

Who it's for

Professionals, business owners, support teams, marketers and agencies that want clearer workflows without constantly switching tools.
