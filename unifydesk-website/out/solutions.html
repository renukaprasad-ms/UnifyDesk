Choose the work your team needs to connect.

UnifyDesk is in development. These examples explain the intended workflows so you can evaluate the workspace, provider prerequisites and team controls before public access opens.

Answer a customer with the earlier conversation in view.

A customer asks about an order on WhatsApp after emailing the company. Support can review available contact and conversation context before answering. The team still needs a clear process for follow-up and an authorized, ready provider connection.

Explore the WhatsApp shared inbox

For enquiries moving between providers, review the omnichannel inbox workflow.

Prepare outreach around a relevant customer group.

Marketing starts with an audience, a purpose and recorded consent. Prepare the message and review provider requirements before sending. A contact list gives the team customer context; it does not automatically grant permission for a campaign.

Give owners a clear view of the operating model.

Business owners need to understand workspace access, provider setup and costs together. Review the platform entitlements the company needs, then account for managed messaging usage and advertising spend separately. Paid plan prices and final limits are coming soon.
