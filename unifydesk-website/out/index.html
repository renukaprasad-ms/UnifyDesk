What is UnifyDesk?

UnifyDesk is a customer communication and marketing workspace. It brings together connected inboxes, contacts and consent, broadcasts, templates, and Google and Meta advertising workflows.

Can I start using UnifyDesk today?

UnifyDesk is under active development. Public sign-up is not open yet. You can explore the product tour and integration roadmap here while we prepare for early access.

Which channels and providers are being supported?

The current development scope includes WhatsApp, email and SMS workflows, Gmail and Microsoft inbox connections, managed email, MSG91 SMS, Google Ads and Meta Ads. The integrations page shows development, planned and evaluation status for each provider.

Are messaging costs included in a plan?

Platform subscriptions cover workspace access and plan entitlements. Managed email and SMS usage are charged separately. Google and Meta advertising spend stays with the connected provider. Public plan pricing is being finalized.

CHANNEL WORKFLOWS

Find the customer workflow your team needs.

Explore conversations and campaign preparation by channel, including provider readiness, consent and the developing product experience.

Omnichannel broadcast India

For Indian marketing, sales, and operations teams who need to send personalized multi-channel campaigns without juggling separate platforms. Manage contact consent, audience segments, approved templates, and live customer replies in one connected system.

WhatsApp shared inbox

For support and operations teams handling enquiries, order questions and follow-ups through a company-owned WhatsApp connection. The focus is the ongoing conversation: who the customer is, what they asked and what the team should do next.

WhatsApp broadcast

For marketing and operations teams planning relevant updates to customers who have agreed to hear from them. Keep audience selection and campaign review separate from the daily work of replying to individual conversations.

SMS marketing

For teams planning permission-based announcements and promotional updates. The workflow brings the customer audience and campaign draft together while keeping sender, template and delivery requirements visible.

Email campaigns

For teams planning newsletters, product updates and other permission-based email outreach. Prepare content and sending readiness together, while keeping connected personal or team inboxes separate from campaign infrastructure.

Omnichannel inbox

For businesses where customer work crosses email, WhatsApp and SMS. The goal is to bring conversations and contact context closer together without pretending that every channel has the same capabilities or delivery rules.
